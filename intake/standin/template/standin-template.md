# Stand-in template — three-section development template

> SYNTHETIC stand-in template for OwnVoice development (build step T4). It exists to
> give the walkthrough a real anti-drift spine on stand-in material. The acceptance
> template (CTU) arrives with `intake/paper-007/` at T6; nothing here is derived from it.

Section order is normative. Each section carries a **type**, and the type is what routes
the section through the loop — never a score (DESIGN §4, frozen decision).

## 1. Why modelled comfort and reported comfort diverge

- **id:** `why-modelled-comfort-and-reported-comfort-diverge`
- **type:** argumentative
- **arc:** 1
- **requirements:** states the disagreement between modelled and reported comfort;
  gives the measured size of the disagreement with its source; names at least one
  mechanism for it; states what the evidence does *not* establish. Every empirical
  claim carries a citation resolvable in `sources/`.
- **must not:** recommend a design intervention (that is section 3's boundary).

## 2. Measurement protocol

- **id:** `measurement-protocol`
- **type:** procedural
- **arc:** 1
- **requirements:** instrument positions with tolerances; settling and logging
  intervals; sampling window per season; calibration before and after deployment with
  the exclusion rule; occupancy counting method; vote-to-interval matching rule. Every
  departure from the cited handbook is recorded with its reason.
- **must not:** argue for the protocol's superiority; state it and justify departures
  only.

## 3. What the evidence base cannot yet settle

- **id:** `what-the-evidence-base-cannot-yet-settle`
- **type:** argumentative
- **arc:** 2 (serial control — walked alone, not batched)
- **requirements:** states each limit of the design; ties each to a source that reports
  the same limit; states the boundary of the claim the study can support.
- **must not:** introduce results not present in section 1 or 2.

## Notes for the walkthrough

- Sections 1 and 2 are the **arc-batched pair**: contrasting types, one sitting, both
  skeletons verified side by side. The measurement that matters is how many
  cross-section findings the author catches only because they sat side by side.
- Section 3 is the **serial control**: same stages, one section end to end, interleaved.
- No administrative section is included; the light path is exercised at T6 where the
  real template has one.
